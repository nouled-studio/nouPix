## What's new in 1.2.2

**sACN**
- nouPix can now send **sACN (E1.31)** instead of Art-Net: **Settings → Output → Protocol**. Art-Net stays the default, and projects from before keep it.
- sACN counts universes from 1, so with sACN every universe is shown, typed and sent that way: Art-Net's universe 0 is sACN universe 1. Switching protocol never moves your patch.
- Send to each controller's address, or to **multicast** groups. A **priority** (0–200, 100 by default) lets a lighting desk take over a universe.
- Receivers are told at once when nouPix stops sending (output off, back to Art-Net, the window closed), and tools like sACNView list the universes nouPix sends.

**More reliable on Windows**
- Security software that filters the network (Avast, Malwarebytes and others) no longer breaks error replies, package downloads or node discovery.
- If a nouPix window disappears without closing properly, nouPix notices within 10 seconds and stops sending its last look to the lights.

**MIDI**
- The MIDI button brings the MIDI window back to the front, also when it's minimised or behind the main window; in the app, **Window → MIDI window** (Ctrl+Shift+M) and **Window → Scenes window** do too.
- The mappings list stays where you scrolled it while a controller sends, and messages in the MIDI window no longer vanish.
- The control you just learnt works straight away, while Learn is still on.
- Learning a control onto one more thing says so; hold **Alt** on the click to replace what it drives instead.
- A pad on a fader (a zone's level, the master, a macro) offers **Toggle** or **Hold**.
- A fader on Pickup shows a white mark where it has to go before it takes over.
- In the patch bay: a group's arrow opens it while learning, **Color 1** and **Color 2**'s own rows can be learnt (their hue), their swatches follow live, and an effect's control shows where its macro takes it.
- A pad's bar drops when you let go, and removing a mapping says Ctrl+Z brings it back.

**Saving in the app**
- Ctrl+S saves straight to the project's file once it has one (saved once, or opened by a double-click), instead of asking every time.

**OMT**
- OMT's library now runs in its own process: if it fails, nouPix keeps running and the stream window says why.

**Smaller things**
- nouPix looks for a new version every time it starts, not just once a day.
- New projects send RGBW white as **W = min(RGB)**, taken off red, green and blue.
- Windows taller than the screen (New playlist with many scenes) scroll, so their buttons stay in view.
