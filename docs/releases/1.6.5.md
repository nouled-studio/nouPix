## What's new in 1.6.5

**Patch**

- **Patch each controller as its device is set up.** Under **Patch by**, a controller is patched by **Universes** per output, by **Pixels** per output, or **Manual**:
  - **Each output its own count:** untick **Same for all** and type each output's universes or pixels on its line, for devices whose ports differ (a DiGidot with 4, 4, 3, 3 universes).
  - **Universes:** outputs follow each other (**In order**), start where you type (**Start typed**, as on an ENTTEC Pixelator), or take the universes you list one by one, in any order (**Each typed**, as on a MADRIX NEBULA).
  - **Pixels:** each output goes on from the channel where the one before ends (**Next channel**, Advatek's Automatic Sequential Channels, WLED), starts on a new universe (**New universe**, SpiNode), or starts where you type (**Typed**, Advatek without Automatic Sequential Channels, Falcon). Next to each typed start, **Follow** and **New U** fill it from the output before. You type pixels and nouPix works out the universes.
  - **Manual:** no outputs. Drag fixtures onto the controller and each gets the next free address. Change it in the list or in the Inspector (**Next free** is there too), or lay several out with **Address in sequence**. Two fixtures on the same channels are shown in red.
- **Counts from 0 or 1:** some devices show Art-Net universe 0 as 1 (Advatek). Set it, and nouPix shows the device's numbers. What is sent doesn't change.
- **Set on the device…** (at the right of the Patch tab) lists what to type into each controller's own setup page: where each output starts and ends, in its own numbers.
- Drop fixtures on a controller in the Patch tab's list: they go at the end of its OUT 1, or to the next free address on a manual one.
- **How patching works** explains the three steps and which mode fits your device. The Patch tab shows the steps until you click **Got it**.
- Projects from before patch exactly as they did.

**Updates**

- **Updates download inside the app:** when a new version is out, Download in the update window now fetches the installer into your Downloads folder without opening a web browser, with a progress bar. When it's there, **Install now** closes nouPix and installs the new version over it (on a Mac, **Open installer** opens the disk image: drag nouPix onto Applications). Your projects and settings stay. This works from this version on, for the updates that come after it.
