## What's new in 1.6.5

**Patch**

- **Patch each controller as its device is set up.** Next to the protocol, **Patch mode** offers three ways (point at each for a word on it, or click the **i** for which fits your device):
  - **Unis:** a number of universes per output. Type each output's start universe and channel on its line (with ▴ ▾); the outputs after it follow on. For DiGidot, ENTTEC Pixelator and Octo, MADRIX NEBULA.
  - **Pix:** a number of pixels per output, and nouPix works out the universes. **Next out** says where each output starts: right after the last one (**Next ch**, Advatek's Automatic Sequential Channels, WLED), on a new universe (**Next uni**), or typed per output (**Manual**, with **Follow** and **New U**). For Advatek PixLite, WLED, SpiNode.
  - **Man:** no outputs. One list of fixtures, each with its own universe and channel on its row. Change one and the others stay put; a fixture moved onto another's channels is said and shown in red. Dropped fixtures take the next free address. For large rigs, a patch copied from elsewhere, DMX fixtures behind a node.
- **Same for all** (ticked at first): untick it to give each output its own count, for devices whose ports differ (a DiGidot with 4, 4, 3, 3 universes). Ticking and unticking loses nothing.
- **Devices found by Discovery are set up for you:** an Advatek asks how it is set up (Pix · Next ch, Pix · Man, Unis) and numbers universes from 1 as Advatek does; an ENTTEC asks Unis or Pix; DiGidot and MADRIX go on Unis, SpiNode on Pix; a WLED patches by pixels only.
- **Universe numbers** (from 0 or from 1, per Art-Net controller) are in **Output settings**. What is sent doesn't change.
- **Skip ch 511–512** is always there, with what it does on pointing at its **i**.
- A fixture dropped on the lower half of another goes after it, so dropping on the last one puts it at the end. Drop fixtures on a controller in the list to add them to it.
- When fixtures of another pixel type go on a Pix controller (RGB onto RGBW), nouPix asks which to change.
- The bin is on the controller's name in the list; it asks first.
- Projects from before patch exactly as they did.

**Tunable white fixtures**

- New pixel types **WC** and **CW**: two channels a pixel, warm and cold white. The content's colour sets the mix: reds and oranges the warm, blues the cold, white and greys both. In the Inspector, **On white** (both full, or sharing one level) and **Balance** (warmer or colder), and a trim per channel.

**Import MadMapper 6 (beta)**

- **Project → Import MadMapper 6 (beta)…** (also on the stage's right-click and + Add): pick a MadMapper project's `.mad` file (in its `.madproject` folder), or a zipped `.madproject`. Its rings, lines and matrices come in fitted to the canvas, with their pixel counts, pixel types, wiring and groups. With **Patch** ticked, each device MadMapper found becomes a Manual controller with its address, and each fixture goes at its universe and channel. **Flip upside down** if it comes in upside down. A beta: check it against MadMapper before a show.

**Updates**

- **Updates download inside the app:** when a new version is out, Download in the update window now fetches the installer into your Downloads folder without opening a web browser, with a progress bar. When it's there, **Install now** closes nouPix and installs the new version over it (on a Mac, **Open installer** opens the disk image: drag nouPix onto Applications). Your projects and settings stay. This works from this version on, for the updates that come after it.
