## What's new in 1.5.1

- **Interact opens in the desktop apps.** The Interact window said to allow pop-up windows and never opened; it now opens as a window of nouPix, like the MIDI and Scenes windows.
- **Pixels on the stage as bright as the content.** With the content shown strongly behind the fixtures, each pixel had a wide dark rim that made it look dimmer than the content around it. Now only a thin outline marks it.

## What came in 1.5.0

**Tempo**
- nouPix listens to a sound input and finds the beat: Settings → Tempo. Choose the input, set the trim, and the BPM shows with a small red dot on each beat and the bar's beats counting (4/4 or 2/4).
- It follows the kick, keeps the tempo through breaks and filtered build-ups, and moves to a new track's tempo after a few seconds.
- A small spectrum shows the band it listens to: drag its edges, or the whole band, to fit the music. Reset puts it back.
- Or tap the tempo, with the Tap button or a MIDI pad. Downbeat puts beat 1 where you want it.
- A small white dot next to the master blinks with the beat; click it to open Tempo.

**Content that follows the music**
- 12 new effects: Beat Pulse, Beat Steps, Beat Strobe, Kick Rings, Spectrum Bars, Spectrum Ring, Level Meter, Bass Bloom, and four atmospheric ones where the music moves one part and the rest drifts (Bass Blobs, Spectrum Veil, Ink Drops, Sound Tide).
- Bass Bloom and Kick Rings listen to the range you choose: the beat band, sub and kick, bass, mids, snare and claps, highs and hats, or everything.
- **Fixture Chase:** lights your groups, or single fixtures, one after another on the beat: every ¼ to 4 beats, forward, back, ping-pong or random, one at a time or filling up, with a fade and a colour. Untick any to leave it out. Put it above other content with Blend: Multiply and it works as a mask: the content shows only on the fixtures whose turn it is.
- Levels follow the music's own range, so quiet music moves the lights as much as loud music.
- Your own ISF shaders can follow the music too, through inputs named `bpm`, `beatPhase`, `barBeat`, `audioBass`, `audioSpectrum` and others.

**Interactive content**
- Five effects follow a finger: Touch Blobs, Touch Clouds, Touch Particles, Touch Ripples and Touch Smoke. Untouched, they drift on their own.
- The finger stirs the air, not paint: a swipe keeps swirling after it lifts and settles slowly. Clouds roll with it, particles gather and turn around the finger, blobs are drawn in and carried off, ripples leave a wake, smoke curls and rises.
- **Interact** in the effect's Parameters opens a black window: drag the mouse, or fingers on a touch screen. The zone's fixtures show faintly on it, so you see where to play.
- On the Remote page, a zone playing interactive content has an **Interact** button: the phone becomes a full-screen pad, with the fixtures on it too. Several phones can play at once.

**Remote page**
- Scenes with a snapshot show it on their buttons, as in nouPix.

**Playlists, simpler**
- No more status text: a tab shows play or pause, the name, the length and how far it is.
- Each step shows its fade and play time: drag sideways to change them, or click to type. Double-click a step to play from it.
- **⋯ → Edit playlist** opens the same window you create playlists with, with the playlist's steps in it.

**Discovery finds WLED**
- A WLED set to E1.31 (sACN) never answered Discovery: it doesn't hear Art-Net's ArtPoll. Discover now also finds WLED devices by their network name and reads how each is set up.
- **Add as controller** fills in a WLED completely: its protocol, universe, start channel and number of LEDs. It warns when WLED is set to DDP or isn't receiving.

**Smaller things**
- Faster drawing of the new effects and of the Fixture Chase.
