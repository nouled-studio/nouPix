## What's new in 1.6.1

**Timeline (Beta)**

- **Keyframes:** unfold a clip (its ▸ or a double-click) and animate any control of its effects over time: numbers and points move straight or eased between keys, colours blend, switches and lists step, a key can hold. Add keys with a double-click or ◆ at the playhead; drag them in time and value; pick several with a box or Shift and move them together.
- **The clip's level as a line on its picture:** double-click the picture to add a key, drag the line between two keys up or down; the picture fades with it, as the light does.
- **The tempo of the music, found by itself:** add an audio file and nouPix finds its BPM and where bar 1 falls; the ruler counts bars from there, faint lines mark every beat, and clips snap to them. Detect finds it again; ×2 and ÷2 fix a count twice too fast or slow; right-click the ruler to start bar 1 somewhere else.
- **Markers on the audio:** a flag with a name (Intro, Build up, Drop, Break, Outro, or your own) and a colour, from + Marker, M or a right-click; click one to go there, drag it to move it.
- **Zoom as in a video editor:** drag the ends of the bar under the tracks to zoom in time, of the bar beside them to make the tracks taller or shorter, their middle to scroll; the sliders stay, and Fit shows everything at once. The audio track folds away when you don't use it.

**Scenes and zones**

- **Three sizes of scene buttons** (small, medium, large), chosen for each place: the bottom panel, either side, the Scenes window.
- **Stop a zone:** its ■ (or Stop in its menu) fades what it plays to black over the default fade and stops its playlist. Phones and tablets can stop a zone too.

**The interface**

- **Side panels' tabs** scroll sideways when they don't all fit, with ‹ › at the end that has more, so a panel moved there is always in reach, its ✕ too.
- **A new tick box:** a thin square with a thin red tick.
- Zone colours now show on the timeline's clips and tracks, as they were meant to.
