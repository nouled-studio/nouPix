## What's new in 1.2.1

**A much faster stage**
- Big projects stay smooth: with thousands of pixels on screen, the stage now shows the content through each pixel's circle instead of drawing every pixel on its own. A 48-universe project that dropped to about 20 frames a second out now holds its frame rate.
- Dimmers, group levels, the master and blackout still show on the stage. Zoomed in close, or while highlighting, each pixel shows exactly the colour it sends.

**Files**
- Project files (`.noupix`) and packages (`.noupixpkg`) have **their own icons** on Mac and Windows; double-click one to open it in nouPix.
