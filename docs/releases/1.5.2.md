## What's new in 1.5.2

**DDP**
- Controllers can now receive DDP, next to Art-Net and sACN: WLED, Advatek, Falcon and others take it. Choose it in the Patch tab.
- DDP has no universes: each controller counts its channels from 1, and its outputs follow on from each other, with no channels skipped. The Patch tab shows each output's and each fixture's channels; in the Inspector you can set where a fixture starts.
- Discovery finds a WLED set to DDP and adds it filled in, with its LEDs and whether they are RGB or RGBW. Its filter now shows All, Art-Net, sACN or DDP.
- Art-Net, sACN and DDP go out together, each controller on its own.

**A steadier output while you work**
- Moving faders, dragging on the stage or playing with interactive content no longer delays the frames sent to the lights.

**Smaller things**
- The pixels on the stage are drawn as before 1.5.1.
