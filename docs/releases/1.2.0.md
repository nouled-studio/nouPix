## What's new in 1.2.0

**Projects that travel**
- **Media library:** images and videos you add are copied once into nouPix's library, so projects open with them every time, with no more relinking.
- **Export package…** (Project menu): one `.noupixpkg` file with the project and all its images and videos, to take to another computer. It shows what goes in and how big it is. Open it with **Open project…**.
- The project's name now follows its file: **Save as** `name2` renames it.

**Shows that start by themselves**
- **Settings → Startup:** open this project when nouPix starts. Made for show computers and Raspberry Pis.
- A scene can **play when the project opens** (⋯ on the scene).
- `noupix.py --project show.noupix` opens a project from a start-up script.

**Video in from the network**
- **NDI and OMT streams** as sources: Resolume, TouchDesigner, OBS, vMix… (**+ Add → NDI or OMT stream…**). NDI needs NDI Tools installed; OMT needs its library (see the user guide).

**Content**
- **21 new effects**: architectural, ambient, disco, and a set after light artists (Turel, Kusuma, Elisun, Flavine, Rilay, Villaray). They all share the same Speed (−1 reverse, 0 still, 1 full) and follow the Color 1 and Color 2 macros.
- **Rainbow and Plasma** can go between two colours of your own.
- A video's **volume can follow its zone's level**, so the X-Fader fades the sound with the picture.
- The **Sources panel** can be made taller by dragging its top edge.

**Smoother**
- Resizing the Scenes panel no longer flashes the stage or stalls the output.
- Moving the X-Fader costs fewer frames: no extra redraws, and the previews no longer make the page wait for the GPU.

**Note:** projects made with older versions ask to relink their images and videos once. After that, the files are in the library.
