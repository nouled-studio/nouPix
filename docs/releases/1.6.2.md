## What's new in 1.6.2

**Timeline (Beta)**

- **Several audio tracks:** a timeline can have as many audio tracks as you need, each on its own row. + Audio adds one at the playhead; a file dropped on the audio rows goes where you drop it.
- **Trim and move:** drag a track's edges to cut the start or end of its file, its middle to move it along the timeline.
- **Volume keyframes:** a track's volume is a line on its waveform. Double-click it to add a key, drag keys or the line between two of them; the waveform dims with it. Each track also has its own volume slider and mute, beside its name.
- Right-click a track to mute it, find the tempo in it, put another file in its place or remove it. Every track goes into packages.
- The tempo of a newly added file is now always found (it could be missed while the file was still being stored).

**Content**

- **Alpha on every colour:** each effect's colours have an Alpha slider in the colour picker. Below 100%, the layers underneath show through: a colour at 0% reveals a solid colour on a lower layer. The swatch in the Content tab shows a checkerboard and the percentage.
- **Rainbow:** always starts as a rainbow and lets you change its colours, from 1 to 5. Only the colours it uses are shown.
- Shorter blend names in the layer list, so a layer's name stays readable.

**The interface**

- **X-Fader beside the stage:** with the scenes in a side panel, the X-Fader's strip has a line of its own across the panel, and every zone has a one-line head.
- Plainer place and size buttons at the foot of the panels.

**Discovery**

- Some nodes set to Art-Net showed as sACN. nouPix now reads the sACN flag from their output ports only. Refresh keeps the protocol you chose, and a device's right-click menu has Use Art-Net and Use sACN.
