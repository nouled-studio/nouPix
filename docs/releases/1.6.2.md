## What's new in 1.6.2

**Timeline (Beta)**

- **Several audio tracks:** a timeline can have as many audio tracks as you need, each on its own row. + Audio adds one at the playhead; a file dropped on the audio rows goes where you drop it.
- **Trim and move:** drag a track's edges to cut the start or end of its file, its middle to move it along the timeline.
- **Volume keyframes:** a track's volume is a line on its waveform. Double-click it to add a key, drag keys or the line between two of them; the waveform dims with it. Each track also has its own volume slider and mute, beside its name.
- Right-click a track to mute it, find the tempo in it, put another file in its place or remove it. Every track goes into packages.
- **Colour keyframes:** each key of a colour is drawn in its own colour. Click one to pick its colour in nouPix's colour picker (wheel, presets, Alpha); the band and the light follow as you pick.
- **Speed keyframes** run from still at the bottom to full at the top, as in the Content tab, so pulling a key up only makes it faster. Any value that crosses zero has a small magnet there.
- **The length** sits right after the time in the bar: type it as 1:30 or 90, or let its button fit it to the end of what is on the timeline.
- **Loop around everything:** one click loops from the first clip, audio track or marker to the last.
- **A new lane goes underneath**, so the clips you already placed stay where they are.
- **A window of its own:** the Timeline, and the Cue lists, can open in their own window, like the Scenes, to put on another screen.
- The tempo of a newly added file is now always found (it could be missed while the file was still being stored).

**Content**

- **Alpha on every colour:** each effect's colours have an Alpha slider in the colour picker. Below 100%, the layers underneath show through: a colour at 0% reveals a solid colour on a lower layer. The swatch in the Content tab shows a checkerboard and the percentage.
- **Faster:** every effect's full speed is now three times what it was. Your scenes play exactly as before; their speed just reads lower on the slider.
- **Rainbow:** always starts as a rainbow and lets you change its colours, from 1 to 5. Only the colours it uses are shown.
- Shorter blend names in the layer list, so a layer's name stays readable.

**A cleaner interface**

- The timeline's bar, the Scenes panel and the cue lists lose their grey boxes: buttons are grey, white when you point at them, and white with a red line when they're on.
- New thin icons for the loop, Fit (on the stage too), markers, audio and snap.
- **Stop a zone** with a small white square that turns red when you point at it.
- **An edited scene** shows a small pencil; click it to overwrite the scene with what plays now (it asks first).
- The place buttons (bottom, left, right, window) are always in the same spot: in the bottom panel's tab row, or next to the open tab beside the stage.
- **X-Fader:** its strip gets a line of its own whenever the panel is narrow, at the bottom or beside the stage.

**Discovery**

- Some nodes set to Art-Net showed as sACN. nouPix now reads the sACN flag from their output ports only. Refresh keeps the protocol you chose, and a device's right-click menu has Use Art-Net and Use sACN.
