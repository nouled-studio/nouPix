## What's new in 1.3.0

**Pitch lock and a strip's angle**
- A strip, ring or arc can **lock its pitch** (the space between its pixels): Inspector → Pixels → Pitch, the padlock next to it. Locked, its length sets how many pixels it has, as with a real LED strip cut to length: drag an end and pixels are added or removed at the same spacing.
- Type a pitch, or step it with ▴ ▾ or the arrow keys; the pixel count stays and the strip gets longer or shorter.
- A strip shows its **Length** and its **Angle** (0° to the right, clockwise). Typing an angle turns it to exactly that direction; Rotate still turns by an amount.

**Dot**
- A new fixture: one pixel drawn as big as the light it stands for (a PAR, a wall washer, a big pixel module). Press **D** and click, once per dot; set its **Size** in the Inspector.

**MIDI Flash**
- A pad on **Flash** plays a scene only while it is held: let go and the zone goes back to what it was playing, its playlist carrying on. A strobe scene under one pad, as on a lighting desk.
- On a level, the master or the X-Fader, Flash is full while held and back where it was; on blackout or a playlist, on while held.

**Pictures on scenes**
- A scene's button can show what it plays: **⋯ → Picture of (zone) as it looks now**. With **Settings → Scenes → Pictures on scenes** on, storing or updating a scene takes one by itself.

**HTTP triggers**
- Show control software, a Stream Deck or a script on the network can play scenes and set zone levels, the master and blackout with a plain web address: **Remote control menu → HTTP triggers…** lists them all, ready to copy. Remote control has to be on.

**Smaller things**
- **Download** in the new-version window opens the page of the latest release.
