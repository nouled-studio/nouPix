## What's new in 1.5.4

**A steadier picture and output**
- The stage now draws in step with the output: a strobe or a fast line looks on screen as it does on the LEDs, instead of flickering at a random rate.
- The layer preview in the Content tab and the source under the mouse take less from the output while they play.

**More room**
- Fold the left panel (fixtures and sources) like the right one: the « in its head, the new button at the left of the stage bar, or Shift H. With both folded, the stage takes the whole width.

**X-Fader**
- An open playlist under zone A or B now uses the whole width of its column.
- The scenes keep their size when the panel's scroll bar comes and goes.
