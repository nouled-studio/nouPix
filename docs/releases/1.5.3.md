## What's new in 1.5.3

**Scenes keep what you change live**
- Change the speed, the colours or a layer's opacity while a scene plays, and the scene keeps it: recall it later and it comes back as you left it. Undo takes it back from both.
- Load other content in a layer, or add or remove a layer, and the scene shows **edited**: click it to save the change into the scene. Recall the scene without saving, and it comes back as it was stored.

**X-Fader**
- With the X-Fader on, the Scenes panel puts zone A's scenes on the left and B's on the right, in 4 columns each that use the width you have. Other zones stay in rows below, and the X-Fader stays at the bottom. With it off, the panel is as before.
- Each zone's level stays live, from its slider, MIDI, the Remote page or an HTTP trigger: it is the most that zone reaches, and the X-Fader mixes within it.
- Turning it on says in one line what it is for, and offers to make both zones full size.

**Smaller things**
- The Channel monitor on a DDP controller shows the channels of what it receives, not universes.
