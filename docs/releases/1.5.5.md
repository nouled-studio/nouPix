## What's new in 1.5.5

- **Cue lists (Beta):** run a show by hand, cue after cue. A cue plays scenes in the zones you choose, takes their levels somewhere and can set an effect's controls (speed, colours…), all over its fade; GO (or Space) plays the cue on standby, and a cue can go on to the next, or to another, by itself after a wait. As many cue lists as you want. This is a beta: try it, and tell us what you miss.
- **Timeline (Beta):** lay scenes along a timeline, a track per zone, in time with an audio file and its waveform. Clips fade in and out (their scene's snapshot fills them), crossfade into each other, and stack on lanes, the top one showing over the ones below. Loop it all or a region; the ruler in seconds or in bars. Also a beta, and it will grow (controls drawn over time are next).
- **Panels where you want them:** the Scenes, Cue lists and Timeline panels can each go to the left or right panel as one more tab (with a ✕ to send it back to the bottom), next to the Inspector, Content and Settings, which stay. The side widens for them, as far as you drag it.
- **The previews in step with the lights:** the stage, a layer's preview, the source under the mouse and the X-Fader's previews all draw on the same frames as the output, up to 60 a second, so a strobe or a fast line looks the same everywhere.
- **The X-Fader's previews** keep moving in the Scenes window while the main window is minimized.
- **The fixture list's tools** (+ Add, SVG…, Group, Lock) sit at its foot.
- **The look:** the left panel's button lights like the right one and sits at the end of the bar, clearer messages (news has a grey line, only errors a red one), the beat band's handles in aluminium, the X-Fader's modes named, and no emoji-like signs.
