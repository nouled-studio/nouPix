## What's new in 1.4.0

**Art-Net and sACN together**
- Each controller has its own protocol, so one show can drive Art-Net and sACN devices at once.
- Projects saved on sACN open with their controllers on sACN.

**Controllers sized by pixels**
- An output can be set by its universes, as before, or by its pixels: give the pixels per output and the pixel type (RGB, RGBW, 16-bit…) and nouPix works out the universes.
- Set the start universe and start channel of each controller. In Pixels mode each output carries on from the channel after the last one of the output before it.

**A simpler Patch tab**
- The controller list shows each controller's name, IP and protocol; one that is off is dimmed.
- Select several controllers with Ctrl or Shift and press Delete. nouPix asks before deleting.
- **Discovery…** next to + Controller finds the devices on the network, with a filter for Art-Net, sACN or both. A device on sACN can now be added even when it reports no Art-Net universes.
- **Output settings** (the cog at the right of the Patch tab): output on or off, the frame rate, ArtSync for Art-Net, and priority and unicast or multicast for sACN. The frame rate is also in Settings → Output.
- **DMX monitor**, next to Output settings, opens in its own window over the app: watch the values while you keep working.
- The Nodes and DMX Monitor tabs, Auto-patch and the extra broadcast address are gone.

**Smaller things**
- New ON/OFF switches, square and minimal.
