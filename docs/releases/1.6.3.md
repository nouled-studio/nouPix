## What's new in 1.6.3

**Macros**

- **Macros for each zone:** every zone now has its own Speed, Intensity, Saturation, Color 1, Color 2 and shared controls. In the MIDI window (and the patch bay), tabs over the macros choose All zones or one zone. Moving a zone's macro changes that zone only; moving All zones' sets it in every zone. Whichever moved last wins.
- A fader learnt on a zone's tab drives that zone's macro. If the same macro is mapped for All zones as well, a short note says so when you make the mapping.
- A small ≠ on an All zones slider tells you some zone has its own value now.
- **Macros act on scenes only:** content you put in a zone but haven't stored as a scene shows its own values, so you can prepare a look without the macros changing it. Once stored, it follows them. The same goes for a MIDI control mapped onto an effect's control.
- **A colour picked for one layer stays that layer's:** picking a colour in the Content tab while its colour macro is on no longer moves the macro (and every other effect with it). The layer keeps its own colour until the macro moves again; a small link next to its name follows the macro again at once.
- **Quieter Content tab:** a control that follows a macro has a thin yellow line under its name instead of a line of text. Point at it to read what it follows.
- Older projects open as before: every zone starts with the macros the project had.

**Tempo**

- **Tapping glides:** tapping a new tempo no longer makes the content jump. It catches up with the new beat within about a beat and a half.
- **T taps the tempo.** Settings → Tempo has a new option, Space taps the tempo, for when you'd rather tap with the space bar.
- **Fixture Chase background:** its background can be a colour, or see-through with its Alpha, so a chase can run over other layers.

**Content**

- **Color rings:** a new effect, Pulse rings in up to five colours of your own.
- **Radar sweep:** the bright line now leads and the glow fades behind it, as on a real radar.

**Discovery**

- **One row per device:** a device that gets a new address is no longer listed twice, and Discover drops devices that no longer answer.
- **Advatek (PixLite…):** adding one on Art-Net explains that Advatek Assistant counts universes from 1, where nouPix shows them from 0.

**Updates**

- **Download gets the right file:** when a new version is out, the Download button now downloads the installer for your computer straight away (Windows, Mac with Apple silicon or Mac with Intel) instead of opening the release page.
- **From the source (Linux, or `python3 noupix.py`):** the window shows the terminal commands to update, with a Copy commands button and a link to GitHub.

**Interface**

- Narrow panels: the X-Fader keeps its size and stays centred, the bottom panel's tabs scroll instead of wrapping, and the cue list fits a narrow panel.
- Small scenes: the zone's head no longer overlaps its buttons.
- MIDI learn: the bar in the main window says it in one short line.
