## What's new in 1.6.4

**Live video in**

- **Camera input:** a webcam or any camera on the computer as a source. Pick the device in the Content tab; it opens while a layer shows it and closes a few seconds after nothing does. Choose its resolution, frame rate and fit.
- **Video input:** a capture card (HDMI or SDI in: Cam Link, Magewell, Blackmagic and the like) as a source, in the same way. Capture cards are recognised by their names, and any device can still be picked by hand.
- **Image controls** for both: flip horizontally or vertically, rotate by 90°, invert, brightness, contrast, saturation and crop.
- A device is remembered by its name, so a project finds it again on another computer. Unplug it and nouPix says so; plug it back in and it comes back.

**Ambilight**

- A new built-in content: choose any source (a video, a camera, a video input, a screen capture, a stream or an effect) and it sits as a picture in the middle of the zone, while the colours of its edges spread outward and soften over the rest. Patch your fixtures around the picture and they glow like the light around a television.
- Sliders for the picture's width and height, what shows in the middle (the picture, its glow or black), depth, segments, softness, fall-off, brightness, saturation and smoothing.

**Sources**

- **Live inputs among the sources:** Camera input, Video input, NDI stream, OMT stream and Screen capture are tiles in the source list, after the built-in effects. Click one to add it and play it. **+ Add** now has the files only (an ISF shader, an image or video).
- The search field has more room under it, and a line the tiles scroll under.

**Patch**

- Channel monitor and Output settings sit in the Patch tab's bar without boxes: a red icon and a grey label.

**Discovery**

- **DiGidot controllers** are now shown on the right protocol. A DiGidot C1 doesn't report Art-Net or sACN the way the Art-Net standard describes (it lists its ports on Art-Net and none on sACN), and nouPix now reads it that way.
